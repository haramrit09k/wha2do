wha2do keeps your day in order with simple, good-looking to-do lists.

• Make a list for anything: groceries, the gym, work, a weekend trip. Each list gets its own icon and colour.
• Add tasks in seconds, with an optional start time so you know what to tackle when.
• Tick tasks off with a tap. Finished tasks drop to the bottom and a progress bar shows how far you are.
• Your lists sync to your account, so they are there when you sign back in.
• Clean, fast, no ads.

## What we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we store:

- **Email address**: to create your account, sign you in and let you reset your password.
- **Username** (the display name you choose) and **phone number**: saved in your profile.
- **Your lists and tasks** (names, optional start times, whether they are done) and when you created them.
- **An account identifier** generated by our authentication provider.

Your **password** is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see it or store it ourselves.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The app contains no
ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

## How the information is used

Only to run the app: sign you in, show your lists and tasks on your devices, and let you reset your password.
We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which acts as our service
provider. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only read and write its own data.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time **inside the app**: open the account icon on the
lists screen and choose **Delete account**. This removes your lists, tasks and profile, and then the account
itself. You can also email [your contact email] from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

## Children

wha2do is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
